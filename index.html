● METHOD EXPLORERPYTHON → SQLITE → DASHBOARD

Make the sample reproducible.

Python generates a fixed-seed CSV and loads it into SQLite. Each dataset has a defined grain of one order or one support ticket.

Write down the calculation.

Net revenue subtracts refunds from gross sales. First-response SLA attainment divides met tickets by met plus breached tickets. Pending tickets stay outside that denominator.

Reconcile totals before reporting.

Retail totals reconcile across months, categories, and channels. The support project reconciles ticket and breach counts and checks tickets at the response deadline.
